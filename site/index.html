    How the war works

    1 · Pick a side

    $BURN lives on Solana, Base, BSC and Robinhood Chain, with its own supply on each. Buy on the chain you fight for.

    2 · Trading feeds the fire

    Every buy and sell pays a 2% fee. On each chain, part of it buys $BURN back and burns it, every 15 minutes, on that chain only.

    3 · Most burned wins

    The chain that trades hardest destroys the most of its own supply. Switch sides any time: bridge your $BURN to another chain on Pangea.

    Burn Wars runs on Pangea. Burns happen on-chain through the coin's own vault. Data from Pangea's public API, refreshed about every 10 minutes. Not financial advice.
